What this proves—and what it doesn't

Demonstrated: This local harness detects mismatches between a model-extracted contract claim and an independently probed implementation, and can refuse an unsafe retry. The simulator deliberately introduced the mismatch.

Not demonstrated: Real-world provider drift, universally reliable idempotency, a statistically powered Muse benchmark, or a new research method. A two-call probe does not establish production guarantees. The guarded actor here is deterministic, not a new Muse continuation.

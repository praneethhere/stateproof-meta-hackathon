Evidence and limitations

Both results came from actual Meta Model API policy responses. The page is a recording, not a live inference endpoint. The test inputs were authored to distinguish negation and guarantee language and do not represent naturally sampled provider docs. A strong deterministic parser or another LLM may perform equally well. The trusted adapter prevents duplicates regardless of incorrect policy recommendations, so do not credit all end-state correctness to Muse.

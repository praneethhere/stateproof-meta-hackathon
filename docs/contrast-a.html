What this experiment does not establish

No new retry protocol, no guaranteed exactly-once semantics, and no claim that Muse outperforms strong baselines. This is one policy candidate on synthetic contracts; production provider semantics require external verification. A one-write hard stop sacrifices completion after a pre-commit failure. A model cannot override the verified adapter.
